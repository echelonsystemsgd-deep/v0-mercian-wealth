Cloud Accounting

Making Tax Digital for Sole Traders and Landlords

Making Tax Digital is changing how sole traders, landlords and businesses report income to HMRC. It has applied since April 2026 to sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000, and extends to over £30,000 from April 2027 and over £20,000 from April 2028. We help you choose the right software, set up digital records and stay compliant ahead of every deadline.

Whether you use Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent, we provide setup support, bookkeeping training and ongoing cloud accounting help to make your records easier to manage throughout the year.
