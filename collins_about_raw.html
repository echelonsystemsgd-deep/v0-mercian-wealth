About Us

Introducing Collins Chartered Accountants

Collins are an independent firm of Chartered Accountants, Tax Specialists and Business Advisors located in Castle Bromwich, Birmingham. Our client base is varied, covering Limited Companies, Partnerships and Sole Traders across a wide range of sectors. No client is too small or too big.

Building & Preserving Personal Relationships

Experience across a wide range of sectors

Currently we work across many sectors including but not limited to Manufacturing, Retail, Property, Construction, Transport, Design and Professionals such as Dentists, Doctors, Solicitors and Architects.

Our Experts

Meet the Team

John Collins FCA Partner

John qualified as a Chartered Accountant with KPMG in 2009 and joined Collins Hart in 2010. He became partner in 2013 when the firm changed name to Collins. He is married to Elle and has a young son called Hudson. Besides family life, interests include endurance events having completed UK Ironman in 2016 and London marathon twice. In addition to surfing, going away in the campervan and supporting Birmingham City. John covers a wide variety of industries and leads all new start-ups for sole traders, partnerships and limited companies. He heads up the journey into the digital era and cloud accounting assisting with transition through Making Tax Digital (MTD). Other areas of expertise include VAT, CIS, income tax and auto enrollment.

Nigel Collins FCA Partner

Having qualified as a Chartered Accountant in the early 1980’s, Nigel has been a partner in professional practices’ for over 30 years. A great deal has changed over that time, but the core role of assisting businesses in financial, taxation and commercial matters remains as relevant today as ever. Away from the office, family, including 2 grandchildren are the main focus, together with following and participating in sport.

Tim Richards ATA

Tax Manager
Tim has specialised in tax for over 35 years having originally trained with KPMG and has worked for large, medium and small practices. Tim is a qualified IFA Tax Adviser and is also a member of the Personal Finance Society and Chartered Insurance Institute (Cert PFS). In his spare time Tim enjoys walking, travelling, watching F1 and socialising.

Garry Harborne FCA

Manager
Qualified in the Midlands with a medium sized accountancy practice. He has a long history in general practice Garry has now worked with Nigel Collins for longer than he chooses to remember. Experienced in a wide variety of accounts related activities. Enjoys watching sport and the outdoors generally.

Eugene McKenna

Andrew Ward
