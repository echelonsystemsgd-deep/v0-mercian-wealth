# Riverside Builders Website Review

## Andrei's Core Observations
- **Not a Real Website, Just a Picture Portfolio**: It functions essentially as an uncurated image gallery connected to a domain name, showing loose pictures without context, explanations, or narrative.
- **No Clear Navigation**: There is no intuitive navigation menu or hierarchy to guide visitors between residential, commercial, or renovation capabilities.
- **No CTA**: Zero action buttons (no "Request a Quote", "Discuss a Build", or "Call Today").
- **Tiny Click Targets**: The tap/click area around buttons and interactive elements is so miniature that it is barely clickable, especially on touchscreens and mobile devices.

## Expanded Forensic Analysis & Commercial Impact
1. **Lack of Conversion Mechanics & Structure**:
   - A business domain cannot rely on visual imagery alone. High-value property clients require proof of process, licenses, insurance, past project briefs, and clear contact avenues.
2. **Missing Touch & Accessibility Ergonomics**:
   - Touch targets below 48x48px violate WCAG standards and cause severe tap frustration on mobile screens, leading to misclicks and immediate user abandonment.
3. **Absence of Clear Commercial Funnel**:
   - Without a clear inquiry button or consultation trigger, visitors who admire the pictures have no defined mechanism to turn into paying leads.
