# Mentor's Strategic Review: Mercian Wealth Offer & Pricing Architecture

**Date**: November 2026  
**Author**: Seasoned Agency Mentor & Systems Architect  
**Audience**: Gurtej Deep (Founder, Mercian Wealth)  
**Tone**: Candid, evidence-led, commercially grounded, no flattery. Plain UK English.

---

## 1. The Mentor's Candid Assessment

Gurtej, let’s be entirely direct. You have made enormous progress transitioning from passive coding into active outbound dialling. You picked up the phone, spoke to managing directors in Berkshire, delivered verified friction points, and had partners confirm live on the phone that their intake forms were broken. That proves you have personal grit and commercial instincts.

However, your pricing structure and commercial packaging currently have friction points that are actively sabotaging your close rate. 

Trying to sell £75/month for *"hosting and maintenance"* is an unforced commercial error. When an accountant hears the word *"hosting"*, their brain looks at their £12/month Squarespace bill or £8/month GoDaddy renewal and concludes they are being overcharged. You cannot win a debate over hosting fees with a Chartered Accountant who reviews overheads for a living.

You must stop selling hosting. You build client acquisition infrastructure, and your retainers must be structured as **Care** (peace of mind infrastructure) and **Growth** (an active pipeline retention engine).

---

## 2. What Is Strong

1. **Niche Selection (1–5 Partner UK Accountancy Practices)**:
   - This is an exceptional target market. Accounting partners are time-poor, compliance-driven, and understand recurring revenue because they live on client retainers. They have healthy cash flow and high customer lifetime values (£2,000–£4,000/yr per limited company).
2. **Outbound Calling Velocity & Conviction**:
   - Dialling live managing directors and delivering human-observable website flaws (e.g. Avery Clifton’s missing form, Jose’s dead booking button, Cryer Sandham’s midday "closed" banner) gives you immediate credibility that 99% of generic spammers will never possess.
3. **Outcome-First Positioning**:
   - Shifting your pitch away from "Next.js speed and tech stacks" towards *"making sure every corporate retainer is captured before they call the next firm"* is the exact language managing partners respond to.
4. **Separation of Build Fee (£595) from Monthly Retainer**:
   - Charging a clean £595 upfront setup fee keeps the barrier to entry low while securing immediate cash flow to fund your business.

---

## 3. What Is Weak

1. **The £75 "Hosting" Trap**:
   - Quoting £75/month on the phone for "hosting and maintenance" makes you sound like a high-friction hosting reseller. Hosting is a commoditised £5–£15 utility. You must never let a debate about server costs kill an £11,900 sales sprint.
2. **Critical Website vs Pitch Contradictions**:
   - If an accountant visits `mercianwealth.com` after your phone call, they see:
     - Setup cards at £595, £1,495, and £4,995.
     - Hosting advertised at **£29/mo** and **£49/mo** (contradicting your £75 phone pitch).
     - Standalone monthly retainers at **£195/mo**, **£495/mo**, and **£995/mo**.
     - An archaic 5%–10% **revenue-share model on digital checkouts** left over from an e-commerce pivot.
     - Contract terms promising *"Zero lock-in contracts; cancel anytime with 30 days notice"*, while on calls you mention a 3-month commitment.
   - *Accountants notice inconsistencies instantly. These contradictions destroy institutional trust.*
3. **The 20-Sales November Goal vs Pipeline Math**:
   - You have **67 leads** in your pipeline.
   - Closing 20 sales from 67 cold leads requires a **29.8% close rate from cold outbound**.
   - In B2B professional services agency sales, standard outbound benchmarks are:
     - Connect rate: 15–25% (10–17 conversations).
     - Discovery booking rate: 20–30% of connects (3–5 meetings).
     - Closed deals: 20–30% of meetings (1–2 closed deals).
   - *Expecting 20 sales from 67 leads is a statistical fantasy.* To hit 20 sales at a healthy 5% lead-to-deal conversion rate, you need a pipeline of **400 verified practice leads**, not 67.
4. **Regulatory Blindspots**:
   - You are processing corporate enquiry data without an active **ICO Data Protection registration** (£35/yr) or a written **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**.
   - You are cold-calling numbers without screening for **TPS (Telephone Preference Service)**. Calling a sole-trader accountant on TPS without prior screening carries statutory ICO fines up to £500,000.

---

## 4. What I Would Change Before Your Next Call

1. **Delete the Word "Hosting" from Your Pitch Body**:
   - Never use the phrase "hosting and maintenance fee". 
   - Default the base proposal to **£595 build + £29/month Practice Care**. That completely neutralises the Wix objection because £29/mo for fully managed UK edge infrastructure is trivial.
2. **Pitch Growth (£75/mo) as an Upgrade Anchor**:
   - Introduce £75/mo strictly as an optional performance engine:
     > *"We have two ongoing options once live: standard Practice Care at £29 a month, which covers your UK cloud infrastructure, security, and updates; or our Practice Growth system at £75 a month, which adds automated 3-touch follow-ups for unbooked leads, instant partner WhatsApp alerts, and a monthly enquiry audit report."*
3. **Ask for Client Value — Never Assert It**:
   - Never say: *"In your sector, missing a lead is £3,000."*
   - Ask: *"Out of interest, what is an average annual fee for a new limited company client on your books?"*
   - When they say *"About £2,500"*, you reply: *"Right. So the £75 Growth engine costs £900 across the entire year. If it recovers just one client who hesitated after hours, it pays for three years of the service."*

## 6. Ten Questions the Founder Must Answer

1. **Scope Boundary**: What is the exact page limit and revision cap for the £595 build? (e.g., maximum 3 core pages, 1 round of consolidation feedback, 48-hour turn).
2. **Lead Replenishment**: Where do the next 200 qualified accountancy practice leads come from once the 67 are finished?
3. **Telephony & WhatsApp Costs**: When an enquiry triggers a WhatsApp or SMS alert to a partner, whose Twilio/Meta API account pays for the messages?
4. **ICO Compliance**: Have you paid the £35 annual ICO Data Protection fee to legally process prospect data?
5. **TPS Screening**: Are you screening practice mobile and landline numbers against the TPS/CTPS register before dialling?
6. **Engagement Contract**: What written Letter of Engagement or Master Services Agreement does the partner sign before paying the 50% deposit?
7. **Payment Rails**: How will you collect the monthly retainer—Stripe card billing (high churn) or GoCardless Direct Debit (industry standard)?
8. **Out-of-Scope Pricing**: If a client on the £29 Care plan asks for a new 4-page service section 3 months in, what is your standard hourly or project rate?
9. **Monthly Report Automation**: How will you compile the monthly Speed-to-Lead report for Growth clients in under 15 minutes without manual spreadsheet data entry?
10. **Offboarding Protocol**: If an accountant cancels their retainer, what exact files do they receive, and who is responsible for repointing their DNS?

## 8. Recommended Canonical Pricing Ladder

### The One-Time Build:
- **Practice Core Build**: **£595** *(or £495 promotional entry rate for fast action)*.
  - Deliverables: Sub-second mobile practice hub (3 core pages), partner contact routing, ICAEW/ACCA/MTD compliance badging, local practice schema, 2-week sprint delivery.
  - Deposit: 50% upfront (£297.50), balance upon final DNS launch.
- **Modular Add-Ons**:
  - Extra Custom Service Page: **£150**
  - Interactive 60-Second Client Triage & 2-Way Calendar Booking: **£300**

---

### The Monthly Retainer Ladder:

| Tier | Price | Primary Deliverables | Target Client |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Practice Care** | **£29 / month** | Managed UK edge cloud hosting, SSL renewals, daily backups, 24/7 uptime monitoring, 1 minor text tweak/mo (up to 20 mins). | Partners who want a fast, secure website with zero maintenance hassle. |
| **Practice Growth** | **£75 / month** | EVERYTHING in Care, PLUS 3-touch enquiry follow-up automation (24h/3d/7d), instant WhatsApp partner alerts, monthly Speed-to-Lead audit report. | Practices actively seeking to grow fees and eliminate unreturned lead leakage. |

*Higher tiers (£125/mo and £200/mo) are completely omitted. Do not offer them until you have 15 active retainer clients.*

---

### Conditions Under Which to Change This Structure:
1. **If > 50% of partners refuse the £29 Care fee**: Package the first 12 months of Care into a **£895 all-in setup fee** (build + 1 year hosting included), then renew at £29/mo in year two.
2. **If > 60% of partners upgrade to Growth**: Increase the Growth tier from £75/mo to **£95/mo** to capture higher recurring margin.
3. **If delivery hours exceed 15 hours per build**: Introduce a strict 3-page scope boundary and mandate a 48-hour client review turnaround.
