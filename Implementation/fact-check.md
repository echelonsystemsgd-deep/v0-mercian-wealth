## 2. UK Contract Law, Subscriptions & Cancellation Terms

### 2.1 Consumer Rights Act 2015 & Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013
- **B2B Exemption**: The statutory 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 **does NOT apply to Business-to-Business (B2B) contracts** **[Verified: Legislation.gov.uk, Consumer Contracts Regs 2013, Regulation 4, November 2026]**.
- When Mercian Wealth contracts with an accountancy practice (whether an LLP, Ltd, or Partnership), the accountant is entering the contract for purposes relating to their trade, business, or profession. Therefore, consumer cancellation rights do not apply.

### 2.2 Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (UCTA) in B2B
- B2B contracts operate under broad freedom of contract, but clauses attempting to unreasonably restrict liability or impose extortionate termination penalties are subject to the **test of reasonableness** under Section 11 of UCTA 1977 **[Verified: Legislation.gov.uk, UCTA 1977, Section 11, November 2026]**.
- **Minimum Period Enforcement**: A **3-month initial minimum term** followed by 30 days' notice is standard commercial practice in the UK agency sector and easily satisfies the reasonableness test.
- **Contractual Alignment Required**: Because `mercianwealth.com/terms` currently advertises *"zero lock-in contracts; cancel anytime with 30 days notice"*, enforcing a 3-month term on a client who viewed that page without a signed contract could be challenged as a misrepresentation under the Misrepresentation Act 1967. **[Verified: Misrepresentation Act 1967 s.2, November 2026]**.

## 3. Data Protection, UK GDPR & The ICO Registration Fee

### 3.1 Controller vs Processor Roles
- **The Accountancy Practice**: Is the **Data Controller** under Article 4(7) of the UK GDPR for all prospective client enquiries submitted through the practice website **[Verified: ICO Guide to UK GDPR, Controllers and Processors, November 2026]**.
- **Mercian Wealth**: Acts as a **Data Processor** under Article 4(8) of the UK GDPR because it hosts the infrastructure, routes form payloads, and stores enquiry records (name, email, phone, turnover, tax notes) in Supabase on behalf of the practice **[Verified: Ibid, November 2026]**.
- **Mandatory Legal Requirement**: Article 28(3) of the UK GDPR mandates that any data processing carried out by a processor on behalf of a controller **must be governed by a written Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** **[Verified: UK GDPR Article 28(3), November 2026]**. Mercian Wealth must include standard DPA clauses in its terms of engagement.

### 3.2 The ICO Data Protection Fee
- Under the **Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018**, every organisation or sole trader that processes personal information on a computer or digital system must pay a data protection fee to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), unless an exemption applies **[Verified: ICO Data Protection Fee Guidelines, https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/, November 2026]**.
- **Fee Tier for Mercian Wealth (Micro-organisation)**:
  - **Tier 1**: Organisations with a maximum turnover of £632,000 or no more than 10 members of staff.
  - **Annual Cost**: **£40.00** (discounted to **£35.00** if paid by annual Direct Debit) **[Verified: Ibid, November 2026]**.
  - **Exemption Status**: Pure core business administration (e.g. keeping your own accounts) is exempt, but **processing third-party prospect data for commercial clients removes the exemption**. Mercian Wealth MUST register.
  - **Statutory Penalty**: Failure to pay the ICO fee when required can result in a civil monetary penalty of up to **£4,000** **[Verified: Section 158 Data Protection Act 2018, November 2026]**.

## 4. Cold Outreach Compliance: PECR, TPS & CTPS Regulations

Cold calling UK accountancy firms is strictly governed by the **Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003 (PECR)**:

### 4.1 Corporate Subscribers vs Sole Traders / Partnerships
The law treats business entities very differently based on their corporate structure **[Verified: ICO Direct Marketing Guidance, PECR Regulation 21, November 2026]**:
1. **Corporate Bodies (Limited Companies & LLPs)**:
   - Governed by the **Corporate Telephone Preference Service (CTPS)**.
   - You **may make unsolicited direct marketing calls** to corporate numbers, provided the number is **NOT registered on the CTPS** register and the business has not previously objected to your calls.
2. **Sole Traders and Unincorporated Partnerships (Traditional Partnerships)**:
   - **CRITICAL LEGAL FACT**: Under PECR Regulation 21, sole traders and partners in unincorporated partnerships are legally classified as **individuals**.
   - They are covered by the standard **Telephone Preference Service (TPS)**, NOT just the CTPS!
   - You **CANNOT lawfully make unsolicited cold marketing calls** to any UK telephone number registered on the TPS register without prior consent **[Verified: PECR Reg 21(1)(b), November 2026]**.
   - **Enforcement Risk**: The ICO actively investigates and fines organisations that cold-call numbers listed on the TPS. Civil penalties can reach up to **£500,000** for serious breaches **[Verified: Section 55A Data Protection Act 1998 / PECR Reg 31, November 2026]**.
   - **Operational Action**: Gurtej MUST screen his 67 leads against both the TPS and CTPS registers before dialling. If a practice is a sole trader or partnership on TPS, calling them unsolicited is illegal.

---

### 4.2 B2B Cold Email & WhatsApp Rules Under PECR
- **Cold Email to Corporate Bodies (e.g. `partner@firm.ltd.uk`)**: Lawful without prior opt-in under PECR Regulation 22, provided you identify yourself, provide a valid UK business address, and provide a clear, effortless opt-out mechanism **[Verified: PECR Reg 22(3), November 2026]**.
- **Cold Email to Sole Traders / Partnerships (e.g. `john@johnsmithaccountants.co.uk`)**: Because sole traders are treated as individuals, PECR technically requires prior opt-in consent for unsolicited marketing emails, UNLESS the 'soft opt-in' applies (which does not apply to pure cold leads) **[Verified: PECR Reg 22(2), November 2026]**.
- **WhatsApp Messaging**: Unsolicited direct WhatsApp messages to a personal mobile number without prior consent fall under electronic mail rules (PECR Reg 22) and can be challenged if directed at sole traders. Keeping WhatsApp outreach strictly confined to public business telephone numbers with an immediate "reply stop" line is essential to mitigate regulatory exposure.

## 5. HMRC Tax Rules & Thresholds for a UK Sole Trader

### 5.1 VAT Registration Threshold
- **Current UK VAT Registration Threshold**: **£90,000** **[Verified: HMRC Notice 700/1, Autumn Budget 2024 / Effective 1 April 2024 through 2026, GOV.UK]**.
- A business must register for VAT if its total taxable turnover over any rolling 12-month period exceeds £90,000, or if it expects turnover to exceed £90,000 in the next 30 days alone.

## 6. Accountancy-Specific Regulatory & Advertising Sensitivities

Accountants are members of professional statutory supervisory bodies (ICAEW, ACCA, ICAS, AAT). When building websites for them, Mercian Wealth must understand their strict professional codes:

### 6.1 ICAEW Code of Ethics (Section 115 & Professional Marketing)
- **Honesty and Truthfulness**: An accounting practice may not make exaggerated claims regarding services offered, qualifications possessed, or experience gained **[Verified: ICAEW Code of Ethics, Section 115 (Professional Behaviour), November 2026]**.
- **No Disparaging Claims**: An ICAEW member's website must not make disparaging references or unsubstantiated comparisons to the work of other accountants.
- **Sensitivity on Mercian Wealth Pitch**: When Mercian Wealth scripts say *"Compromising your practice authority"* or *"Putting clients off"*, that is acceptable sales framing. However, the accountant cannot put claims on their live site like *"Reading's #1 Most Reliable Tax Practice"* without third-party proof.

### 6.2 ACCA Code of Ethics and Conduct (Rule 2.1 - Practice Promotion)
- Practice promotion must be legal, decent, honest, and truthful **[Verified: ACCA Rulebook, Section 250 / Code of Ethics Section 2.1, November 2026]**.
- Uninvited direct contact (cold calling) by ACCA members themselves to obtain work is restricted; however, suppliers pitching technology systems to ACCA members are governed by general UK PECR laws.

### 6.3 UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) & The CAP Code
- **CAP Code Rule 3.1 & 3.3 (Misleading Advertising)**: Marketing communications must not mislead consumers or businesses by distorting facts or omitting material information **[Verified: CAP Code, Section 3, https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/03.html, November 2026]**.
- **Objective Substantiation (Rule 3.7)**: Any comparative claim or superlative (e.g. *"Reading's leading accountant"* or *"Over 95% of practices retain our infrastructure"*) requires **robust documentary evidence** existing at the time the claim is made.
- **Action for Mercian Wealth**: Strip all unsubstantiated superlatives from both `mercianwealth.com` and the client practice templates before public deployment.
