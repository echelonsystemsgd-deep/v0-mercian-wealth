## 1. Strategy & Non-Negotiable Operating Rules

### 1.1 The High-Fee Client Reality
* An average UK limited company client is worth **£1,500 to £3,500+ per year in recurring fees**.
* High-intent business directors searching for an accountant do not browse 10 tabs for hours. They enquire at evening or weekend hours and sign with whoever responds with clarity, speed, and professionalism.
* **78% of prospective business clients sign with the first firm that responds.**
* If an accounting practice takes 24–72 hours to reply to a web contact form, they are leaking £6,000 to £15,000+ in annual recurring revenue without even realising it.

### 1.2 The Two Fatal Traps to Avoid
1. **The Hostinger / Cheap Website Trap:**
   * *The Mistake:* Arguing over £500 brochure sites or competing with cheap website builders.
   * *The Rule:* Never quote design fees before diagnosing operational leakage. Anchor all conversations against lost client revenue (£1,500–£3,500/year per client) and 10+ partner admin hours saved per week.
   * *The Pivot Line:* *"We do not build generic brochure websites. We engineer practice intake and deadline chasing infrastructure that captures £3k/year clients before they bounce and protects your capacity ahead of MTD 2026."*

2. **The Xero / Custom Software Trap:**
   * *The Mistake:* Promising custom software that replicates Xero, IRIS, BrightManager, or Dext.
   * *The Rule:* Never attempt to replace core accounting ledgers. The infrastructure operates purely at the **front-door (intake and booking)** and the **communication cadence layer (pre-approved deadline chasing)**.
   * *The 1-Question Clarifier:* *"What single day-to-day bottleneck are you trying to eliminate for your team right now?"*

### 1.3 Governance & Compliance Mandates
* **Practitioner-in-the-Loop:** Reminders and intake workflows are pre-approved. Partners and practice managers retain complete control to pause, edit, snooze, or override any client cadence.
* **Zero Confidential Ledger Access:** Systems manage appointment holds, qualification status, and document reminders. They never interface with or store sensitive client ledgers, bank feeds, or tax computations.
* **UK GDPR Compliant:** Explicit consent capture, frictionless opt-outs, and zero training of AI models on practice client data.

## 3. The 3-Minute Practice Response Speed Test Diagnostic Checklist

Use this 5-point diagnostic protocol before recording a Loom or sending a personalised outreach pitch. Takes exactly 180 seconds.

| # | Audit Item | Testing Method | Red Flag (Revenue Leak) | Green Flag (Modern Infrastructure) |
|---|------------|----------------|-------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **1** | **Mobile Ergonomics & Speed** | Load website on smartphone on 4G network. | Takes >3s to load; form fields require pinch-to-zoom; tiny buttons. | Sub-second load; 44px+ touch targets; thumb-friendly tap intake. |
| **2** | **Out-of-Hours Enquiry Triage** | Inspect contact page at 8 PM. Submit sample test or review options. | Generic `info@` email or static form; promises "We will reply in 2–3 business days." | 3-minute smart intake; instant calendar hold; conditional qualification. |
| **3** | **Client Qualification Depth** | Check if form asks key qualification questions. | No turnover or entity filter; invites unqualified tyre-kickers to take phone slots. | Captures Ltd Co / Sole Trader / Turnover tier upfront before booking call. |
| **4** | **MTD & Record Chasing Readiness** | Check site messaging around MTD for ITSA 2026. | Zero mention of quarterly reporting; manual email/phone chasing required. | Pre-approved automated reminder cadences; client upload portal links. |
| **5** | **Onboarding Speed** | Review client onboarding path. | Manual PDF engagement letters; manual AML identity chasing taking 3+ weeks. | Frictionless 48-hour digital onboarding checklist (AML, 64-8, Direct Debit). |

### Diagnostic Scoring Matrix
* **0–2 Green Flags (Critical Revenue Leakage):** Firm is actively bleeding £1,500–£3,500/year clients to competitors. Prime candidate for Touch 1 Out-of-Hours Hook.
* **3–4 Green Flags (Operational Bottleneck):** Firm has traffic but staff is burning 10–15 hours/week on administrative drag. Pitch the MTD Chasing & Capacity Calculator.
* **5 Green Flags:** Highly modernised firm. Candidate for advanced automated workflows.

## 5. Discovery Call Question Flow & Qualification Protocol

### The 1-Question Clarifier (Non-Negotiable)
When an accountant asks: *"Can you build a portal that integrates with our custom legacy database and does everything Xero does?"*  
**Ask this immediately:**
> *"What single day-to-day bottleneck are you trying to eliminate for your team right now?"*

---

### The 5-Phase Discovery Call Blueprint (15–20 Minutes)

#### Phase 1: Context & Practice Anatomy (0–4 mins)
* Goal: Understand firm size, entity split, and average fee.
* Questions:
  1. *"To make sure we're focused on what matters: roughly how is your client base split right now between Limited Companies, Sole Traders, and Landlords?"*
  2. *"What does your average annual fee look like for a full Ltd Co compliance & advisory client? Is it around £2,000–£3,500?"*
  3. *"How many fee-earners or client managers are currently on the team?"*

#### Phase 2: Bottleneck Diagnostic & Revenue Leakage (4–8 mins)
* Goal: Uncover the friction in enquiry response and document chasing.
* Questions:
  1. *"When a new enquiry comes in through your website today, what happens step-by-step between them clicking submit and speaking to you?"*
  2. *"How many unreturned enquiries or slow replies happen simply because the team is heads-down during VAT or Self Assessment season?"*
  3. *"With MTD for ITSA forcing sole traders and landlords over £50k to report quarterly from 2026, how much time is your team currently spending chasing records and receipts?"*

#### Phase 3: Governed Solution Walkthrough (8–13 mins)
* Goal: Walk them through `mercianwealth.com/practices#practice-visualizer` live.
* Focus points:
  1. **Stage 01:** Show how a Ltd Co director qualifies themselves in 3 minutes and books a slot.
  2. **Stage 02:** Show the MTD Deadline Chaser. Highlight the **Batch Approval** and **Practitioner-in-the-Loop** toggles. Emphasise: *"Your team approves every message. Nothing sends without your sign-off."*
  3. **Stage 03:** Show the 48-Hour Onboarding Checklist. Contrast 3 weeks of email chasing with automated AML and 64-8 authorisation.
  4. **Stage 04:** Review the Capacity Calculator (11.5 hours saved per partner per week).

#### Phase 4: Financial Anchoring & Disqualification (13–16 mins)
* Goal: Establish ROI and disqualify budget-seekers.
* Questions:
  1. *"If this system helped you capture just 2 additional Ltd Co clients a year who would have otherwise bounced to a faster-responding firm, that's £6,000+ in annual recurring fees. Does that align with how you look at the investment?"*
  2. *"If the system recovers 10 hours a week across your team ahead of MTD, what would that extra fee-earner capacity mean for your billing?"*
* **Anti-Hostinger Guardrail:** If they ask: *"Can't I just get someone on Fiverr or Hostinger to make a £300 site?"*  
  *Response:* *"You absolutely could if you just need an online business card. But a brochure site won't qualify Ltd Co directors at 9 PM, won't collect upfront booking commitment, and won't chase MTD records. We engineer conversion and compliance infrastructure. If a brochure site is all you're looking for, we're likely not the right fit."*

#### Phase 5: Next Steps & 7-Day Deployment Scope (16–20 mins)
* Deployment protocol:
  1. Day 1: 30-minute scoping call (booking links, qualification criteria, entity thresholds).
  2. Days 2–5: Infrastructure build, mobile testing, and compliance verification.
  3. Day 6: Team review & approval walkthrough.
  4. Day 7: Live deployment with zero disruption to existing Xero / IRIS / Dext stack.
