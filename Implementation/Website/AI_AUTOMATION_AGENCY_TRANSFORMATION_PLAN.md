**Core Offerings**:  
1. Chatbots & Automated Customer Support (Instant 24/7 enquiries)

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
The website previously over-indexed on generic website-building and retained legacy bakery/cafe remnants (such as *"menu updates"*, cake orders, pickup dates). This plan converts the entire digital presence into a world-class, enterprise-credible AI Automation Agency platform.

### Key Objectives:
1. **Total Copy & Brand Congruence**: The site 100% mirrors what the founder pitches on discovery calls.
2. **Purge All Food/Bakery Relics**: Zero mentions of menus, baked goods, or single-niche retail constraints.
3. **Interactive Visual & Audio Verification**:
   - Audio Voice Demo player (Inbound Voice Receptionist + Outbound Lead Reactivation).
   - Interactive Automation Workflow Visualizer.
   - Retaining the interactive ROI calculator and direct WhatsApp lead flows.
4. **Mobile-First Excellence**:
   - 100% responsive, touch-friendly UI, zero horizontal overflow, fluid font scaling, lightweight animations.
5. **Rock-Solid Code Quality**:
   - Zero TypeScript, Next.js, or ESLint build errors.
