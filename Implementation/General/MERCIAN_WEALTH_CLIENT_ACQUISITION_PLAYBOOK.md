# 🏛️ Mercian Wealth — High-Ticket Client Acquisition Playbook

Use this standardized, battle-tested playbook for any local trade contractor, bespoke retail jeweller, clinic, salon, or high-ticket service business.

Simply replace the bracketed variables (e.g. `[BUSINESS_NAME]`, `[CITY]`, `[CORE_OFFERING]`) when pitching a new prospect.

---

### Core Pitching Principles
1. **Sell the Outcome, Never the Tool:** Never mention code, React, Next.js, WordPress, or design plugins. Talk exclusively about **speed-to-lead**, **capturing evening mobile searches**, **eliminating no-shows via deposits**, and **stopping leads leaking to competitors**.
2. **The Unit Economics Anchor:** Always demonstrate that **1 to 2 customer orders/jobs** completely pays off the entire build fee.
3. **Dual Commercial Paths:** Give every prospect the choice between **Complete Asset Ownership** (one-off capital asset) and a **Fully Managed Retainer** (100% hands-off).
4. **High-Status Scarcity:** Never chase. If they decline, calmly revoke access and introduce 48-hour regional exclusivity (giving the catchment slot to their competitor).
