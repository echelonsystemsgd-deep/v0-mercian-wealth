# 🏛️ Mercian Wealth — High-Ticket Client Acquisition Playbook

Use this standardized, battle-tested playbook for any local trade contractor, bespoke retail jeweller / diamond atelier, clinic, salon, or high-ticket service business.

Simply replace the bracketed variables (e.g. `[BUSINESS_NAME]`, `[CITY]`, `[CORE_OFFERING]`) when pitching a new prospect.

---

### Core Pitching Principles
1. **Sell the Outcome, Never the Tool:** Never mention code, React, Next.js, WordPress, or design plugins. Talk exclusively about **speed-to-lead**, **capturing evening mobile searches**, **eliminating no-shows via deposits**, and **stopping leads leaking to competitors**.
2. **The Unit Economics Anchor:** Always demonstrate that **1 to 2 customer orders/jobs** completely pays off the entire build fee.
3. **Qualify Before Delivering:** When the prospect agrees to see a prototype, execute the 2-minute diagnostic first. This anchors their ticket economics, quantifies their monthly bleed, and gets them to pre-close themselves before they ever see the link or price tag.
4. **Dual Commercial Paths:** Give every prospect the choice between **Complete Asset Ownership** (£995 one-off capital asset) and a **Fully Managed Retainer** (£495 setup + £195/mo rolling).
5. **Machiavellian Scarcity:** Never chase. If they decline or stall, calmly revoke access and introduce 48-hour regional exclusivity (giving the catchment slot to their nearest competitor).

## 🔥 STAGE 2: The Revenue Leakage Qualifying Diagnostic
*(Execute immediately when they agree to see the prototype — BEFORE building or sending the link)*

> Brilliant. Before I put it together on my end — I don't build generic cookie-cutter templates. I engineer each system specifically around the exact customer journey and economics of **[BUSINESS_NAME]**.
>
> Quick 2-minute diagnostic so I get the prototype 100% dialled in for you:
>
> 1. **High-Ticket Focus & Ticket Anchor:** What's your primary high-ticket focus right now — is it bespoke commissions / custom pieces, or finished showroom stock? And roughly what is an average commission/order worth to you in gross revenue?  
>    *(Target: "Mainly bespoke commissions, around £2,500–£5,000")*
>
> 2. **Booking & Commitment Model:** When a client wants to discuss a bespoke ring or high-value piece, do they currently just send a DM or drop in, or do you have a dedicated private showroom consultation booking where they can secure their slot or pay a holding deposit?  
>    *(Target: "They usually just DM / drop by, we don't have a deposit system")*

> 3. **Sunday Evening Reality:** Right now, over 75% of high-intent searches in **[CITY]** happen on smartphones in the evening or on weekends when the showroom is closed / shop is shut. When someone discovers **[BUSINESS_NAME]** on Sunday at 9 PM, what actually happens? Can they explore your work, build confidence, and lock in a consultation, or do they bounce to whoever answers online first?  
>    *(Target: "They probably bounce / message someone else / wait until Monday")*
>
> 4. **Quantifying Monthly Bleed:** Realistically, how many potential high-ticket enquiries or clients do you think slip through the cracks each month simply because there's no fast digital storefront capturing them outside working hours?  
>    *(Math Anchor: 2 missed orders × £2,500 = £5,000/month or £60,000/year in leaking revenue).*  
>    *(Target: "Probably 2 or 3 a month")*
>
> 5. **The 1-Job Payoff Pre-Close:** So if having this dedicated system captures literally ONE single customer over the next 90 days that would have otherwise gone to a competitor — that completely pays off the entire project, and everything after that is pure profit, right?  
>    *(Target: "Yeah, absolutely.")*
