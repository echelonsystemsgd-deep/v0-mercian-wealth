### 2. Multi-Stage Cold Outreach Strategy (Mercian Wealth SOP)

#### Stage 1: The Pattern Interrupt (0% Sales Pitch)
> `"Hi 👋"`
* **Why it works:** It feels like a real customer inquiring about a quote. 90% of trade owners respond within 15 minutes.

#### Stage 2: The Demonstrative Pitch (Value Before Money)
> *"Came across [Business Name] — had a look for a website, couldn't find one. Here's the bit that actually matters: every day someone searches for a [Trade] near them, sees you're not there, and books with whoever showed up instead. That's not a maybe — it's happening right now, on jobs you'll never even know you lost.*
> 
> *I build the storefront before I ever ask for money — I don't sell direction I haven't already shown. If it's not for you, no pressure at all, just glad I flagged it. If it is, we take it from there."*

#### Stage 3: Polite Follow-up (Day 2)
> *"Hey — following up on [Business Name]'s site. No pressure at all, just wanted to check it landed and see where you're at."*

#### Stage 4: Clean Fair Close (Day 4)
> *"Last one from me on this — if it's not a priority right now, that's completely fair. If it ever is, the offer stands, just say the word."*
