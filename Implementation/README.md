## 🏛️ Canonical Brand Tokens

* **Canvas Charcoal**: `#09090B` (Primary canvas background)
* **Surface Charcoal**: `#111115` (Cards, dialogs & containers)
* **Elevated Charcoal**: `#18181F` (Input fields, dropdowns & elevated surfaces)
* **Tech Accent Gold**: `#DCAE53` (Primary CTA buttons, badges & active states)
* **Border Line**: `border-slate-800` / `border-[#DCAE53]/30` (Hairline separation)
* **Emblem Mark**: Geometric Hourglass/Bowtie MW Mark (`/public/Logo1.jpeg` & `/public/Logo2.jpeg`)
* **Typography**: Geometric sans-serif (`Inter`, `Geist`) and monospace (`Geist Mono`). Zero serif fonts.
