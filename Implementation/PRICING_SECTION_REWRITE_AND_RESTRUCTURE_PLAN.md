# Pricing Section Rewrite & Restructure Implementation Plan

**Date**: September 2026  
**Status**: Approved & Executed (Grounded Deliverability & Enforceable Gateway Attribution)  
**Scope**: Complete rewrite and restructure across all three pricing models: **System Build (One-Time Setup)**, **Revenue Share (Performance Partner)**, and **Growth Retainer (Managed Scope)**.

---

## 1. Objectives & Executive Summary

1. **Clean, Honest, and Plain Business English**:
   - Focus strictly on client outcomes: inquiries, automated booking dispatches, operational clarity, and discoverability.
   - Eradicate developer jargon and eliminate all generic filler copy.
2. **Zero Timeframe Mentions & Strict Zero-Fabrication Compliance**:
   - Eliminate all arbitrary turnaround claims ("48-hour delivery", "30-day support", "60-day support", "24/7 dedicated engineering").
   - Frame delivery around agile sprint milestones agreed upon kickoff.
3. **Grounded Search Deliverables (Schema.org / No Unverifiable AI Hype)**:
   - Avoid unverifiable claims regarding "AI search engine recommendations" or "generative engine domination".
   - Ground search deliverables strictly in verified **Technical SEO and Schema.org structured data (JSON-LD)** for Google Search, rich snippets, and machine-readable search crawlers.
4. **Enforceable Revenue-Share Digital Gateway Attribution (Tab 2)**:
   - Revenue share (5%, 8%, 10%) is strictly tied to **online bookings, customer deposits, and checkout orders processed directly through the integrated website payment gateway**.
   - Offline, telephone, in-store, and cash transactions remain 100% the client's and are never tracked or billed.
5. **Domain & Hosting Transparency (Tab 1)**:
   - Clear, empowering notice on every tier: client pays domain and hosting costs directly to their chosen provider (e.g. £5–£15/mo).
   - Zero vendor lock-in; 100% intellectual property and asset ownership retained by the client.
6. **Realistic, Deliverable Growth Retainer Pricing (Tab 3)**:
   - Converted from `"Managed Scope"` to verified, profitable, and accessible monthly investments:
     - **Launch Support**: **£195/month** (Billed monthly · Zero lock-in contracts)
     - **Leverage Growth**: **£495/month** (Billed monthly · Zero lock-in contracts)
     - **Enterprise Alliance**: **£995/month** (Billed monthly · Zero lock-in contracts)
   - Feature scope scoped strictly to deliverable maintenance commitments (hosting oversight, Search Console health checks, intake maintenance, monthly tweaks).
7. **Custom Scope Options on Every Tab**:
   - Every tab includes an explicit Bespoke / Custom Scope block for companies requiring non-standard requirements.
8. **Single Unified CTA**:
   - Every tier and custom scope card links strictly to `/book` with the label **"Book a call"**.
9. **Mobile-First Responsive Engineering**:
   - Audited for zero horizontal overflow, seamless card stacking, touch-target compliance (min 44px), and legible typography across all mobile viewports (320px–425px).

## 4. Mobile Responsiveness Quality Assurance Standards

- Touch targets minimum 44px × 44px (`min-h-[44px]`).
- Clean font hierarchy with responsive sizes (`text-xs sm:text-sm md:text-base`).
- Tab switcher wraps cleanly with horizontal scrolling protection (`overflow-x-auto scrollbar-none shrink-0`).
- Horizontal padding constrained to avoid mobile side-scrolling (`px-2 sm:px-6`).
- Card padding optimized for narrow screens (`p-4 sm:p-7`).
- All interactive elements use standard native button semantics for mobile Safari & Chrome.
