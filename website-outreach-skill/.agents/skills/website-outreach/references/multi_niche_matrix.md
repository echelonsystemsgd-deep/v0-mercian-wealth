# Universal Multi-Niche Flaw & Forensic Inspection Matrix

This master matrix equips the AI to perform deep, surgical audits across any commercial business niche. Different businesses suffer from different commercial leaks—your AI must evaluate websites through the lens of that specific industry's buyer journey.

---

## 1. Accounting, CPAs & Tax Advisory
- **Core Buyer Intent**: Tax liability reduction, HMRC penalty avoidance, corporate compliance, peace of mind.
- **Top 4 Conversion Killers**:
  1. *Static Form vs. Live Booking*: Requiring 8-field forms with 48h turnaround instead of instant intake scheduling.
  2. *Menu Keyword Dump*: 20+ all-caps links (`CIS`, `PAYROLL`, `MTD`) causing cognitive overload.
  3. *Missing Mobile `tel:` Link*: Forcing mobile directors to memorize or copy-paste telephone digits.
  4. *Buried Badges*: Hiding ICAEW, ACCA, AAT, CTA, Xero Platinum, or QuickBooks badges.
- **High-Converting Outreach Angle**: *"We help firms stop losing lucrative corporate retainers to whoever replies to contact form submissions first."*

---

## 2. Trades, Construction & Contractors (Builders, Roofers, Landscapers)
- **Core Buyer Intent**: Trust, upfront pricing clarity, emergency speed, proof of build craftsmanship.
- **Top 4 Conversion Killers**:
  1. *Uncurated Photo Album Trap*: A collection of loose images with no project specs, build scope, or customer narrative (e.g. Riverside Builders).
  2. *Wall of Text & Header Stacking*: 3–4 stacked header bars pushing services below the fold and dense text blocks (e.g. G Builders).
  3. *Unclickable Mobile Elements*: Touch targets under 48px making emergency quotes impossible on phones.
  4. *Incoherent Copy & Pricing Typos*: Mixing currencies (£ vs $) or quoting nonsensical pricing brackets that destroy trust.
- **High-Converting Outreach Angle**: *"We help quality builders showcase verified project results so homeowners stop bouncing to cheaper cowboy contractors."*

---

## 3. Private Medical, Dental & Aesthetic Clinics
- **Core Buyer Intent**: Safety, credential reassurance, discrete booking, visual before/after proof.
- **Top 4 Conversion Killers**:
  1. *Treatment Accordion Burial*: Hiding consultation pricing and recovery details under collapsed, unsearchable menus.
  2. *No Instant Deposit / Appointment Slot*: Forcing prospective patients to send an email inquiry rather than locking in a consultation time with a secure card deposit.
  3. *Buried CQC / GDC / GMC Numbers*: Failing to place regulatory compliance badges prominently in the header/hero.
  4. *Slow Media Hydration*: High-resolution uncompressed treatment photos causing 4+ second layout jumps on mobile.
- **High-Converting Outreach Angle**: *"We help private practices capture upfront consultation deposits on mobile rather than losing patients to clinics with instant booking."*

---

## 4. Legal & Commercial Advisory (Solicitors & Law Firms)
- **Core Buyer Intent**: Case outcome certainty, partner caliber, immediate fee transparency or confidential intake.
- **Top 4 Conversion Killers**:
  1. *Generic Slogan Hero*: Passive statements like *"Justice for our clients"* with zero explanation of practice area specialties.
  2. *Intimidating Legal Jargon*: Greeting visitors with academic legal statutes rather than solving the client's concrete crisis.
  3. *Missing Confidential Intake Flow*: Standard insecure forms lacking clear GDPR/client-attorney confidentiality reassurance.
  4. *Buried SRA / Law Society Badges*: Missing statutory regulatory entity numbers in the footer.
- **High-Converting Outreach Angle**: *"We help law firms turn urgent commercial inquiries into booked consultations before the prospect consults rival counsel."*

---

## 5. Destination Dining, Boutique Hotels & Hospitality
- **Core Buyer Intent**: Sensory visual appeal, menu transparency, dietary confidence, 2-click table/room reservation.
- **Top 4 Conversion Killers**:
  1. *PDF Menu Trap*: Forcing mobile diners to download a 15MB PDF menu that cannot be read on small screens without pinching.
  2. *Broken Reservation Flow*: Multi-step OpenTable or SevenRooms embeds that freeze or clip within an iframe.
  3. *Hero Banner Without Atmosphere*: Stock food photography that looks artificial and fails to convey the venue's interior ambiance.
  4. *Hidden Opening Hours & Location*: Burying parking, address, and live kitchen hours deep in a footer link.
- **High-Converting Outreach Angle**: *"We help destination restaurants replace awkward PDF menus with mobile booking flows that stop diners bouncing to OpenTable competitors."*

## 6. The Forensic 4-Pillar Website Evaluation Standard

When running an audit under this matrix, evaluate all four layers:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. VISUAL LAYER                                        │
│   • Hero alignment & 5-second clarity                  │
│   • Desktop hamburger / sandwich menu anti-patterns    │
│   • Touch target sizes (min 48x48px)                   │
│   • Visual hierarchy & whitespace discipline           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. COPY & MESSAGING LAYER                              │
│   • Specificity Test: Does it state who, what & outcome?│
│   • Banned Cliches: "Excellence in service", "Trusted" │
│   • Grammar, currency, and punctuation integrity        │
│   • Tone: Commercial confidence without arrogance      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. CONVERSION & FUNNEL LAYER                           │
│   • Single primary CTA hierarchy ("Book a call")       │
│   • Instant calendar / WhatsApp vs slow static forms   │
│   • Risk reversals, guarantees & objection handling FAQs│
│   • Prominent reviews & named client social proof      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. TECHNICAL & STRUCTURAL LAYER                        │
│   • Clean semantic H1 (no blank <h1>&nbsp;</h1> tags)   │
│   • Mobile tap-to-call (tel:) links                     │
│   • Zero theme demo remnants (ThemeForest leftovers)   │
│   • Zero broken JS buttons or raw template placeholders│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
