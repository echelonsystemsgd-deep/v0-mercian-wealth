# K S Rathod & Co Website Review

## Core Observations
- **Dated Web 2.0 Layout & Hardcoded Formatting**: The header uses primitive raw non-breaking space characters (`&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;`) hardcoded into the HTML markup to force spacing between the email and phone number.
- **Accidental Text Duplication**: The slogan *"The freedom to grow your business"* is printed twice consecutively in the main hero viewport.
- **Weak Single-Word CTA**: The primary contact trigger is an unstyled, single-word button labeled simply `"Contact"` with zero contrast or value incentive.
- **Missing Touch Ergonomics**: Elements sit crowded with awkward white gaps and no mobile thumb-zone layout.

## Commercial Flaw & Outreach Angle
- **Observed Problem**: The hero banner contains hardcoded raw spacing hacks and repeats the same slogan twice next to a flat "Contact" button.
- **Recognizable Reason**: Visitors scanning the firm see amateurish visual formatting that looks like an unmaintained site from 2012.
- **Consequence Mechanics**: High-net-worth business clients seeking premium chartered accounting support question the firm's modern operational standards and look elsewhere.
