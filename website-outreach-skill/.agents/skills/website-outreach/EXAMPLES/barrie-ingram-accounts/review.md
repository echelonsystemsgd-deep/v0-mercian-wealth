# Barrie Ingram Accounts Website Review

## Core Observations
- **Duplicate Navigation & Mobile Bloat**: The navigation menu renders duplicated links in the header (`Home About Us Blog Testimonials Services...` appears twice in the DOM), creating visual noise.
- **Vague Multi-CTA Clutter**: Contains 4 identical, undescriptive "FIND OUT MORE" buttons rather than clear commercial actions tied to specific service lanes (e.g. "Request CIS Quote", "Limited Company Intake").
- **Generic Slogans**: Uses passive boilerplate headlines (*'Talking your language - "We are here to help you"'*) rather than stating concrete financial or tax outcomes.

## Commercial Flaw & Outreach Angle
- **Observed Problem**: The homepage displays repetitive generic "FIND OUT MORE" buttons across every service card without a direct inquiry mechanism.
- **Recognizable Reason**: Prospective business owners looking for CIS or limited company accounting have to click through multiple vague sub-pages just to find how to initiate contact.
- **Consequence Mechanics**: High-intent trade contractors and company directors abandon the research phase and dial a competitor who offers instant pricing or an intake form.
