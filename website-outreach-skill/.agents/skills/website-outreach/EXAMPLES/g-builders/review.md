# G Builders Website Review

## Andrei's Core Observations
- **Wall of Text & Random Pictures**: Users are immediately confronted with dense paragraphs and disconnected images under a generic "Why Choose Us" header, offering no cohesive narrative or intuitive journey.
- **Header Stacking Nightmare**: The page features 3 stacked headers and a 4th header/title box pushing all meaningful content and services deep down the screen.
- **No CTA**: Missing any prominent, contrast-rich call to action button above the fold.
- **Incoherent & Damaging Copy**: Unprofessional and contradictory claims like: *"G builders has successfully completed projects ranging from £150 million to £1500- dollars"* — mixing currency symbols, bizarre pricing brackets, and formatting errors that destroy trust.

## Expanded Forensic Analysis & Commercial Impact
1. **Severe Credibility Damage via Copy Errors**:
   - High-ticket construction clients looking to spend substantial sums will not trust a company whose copy confuses pounds with dollars and quotes absurd price spans (£150M to $1,500).
2. **Above-the-Fold Real Estate Waste**:
   - Having 3–4 stacked header bars clutters the viewport and pushes the actual portfolio and service descriptions below the fold, causing instant cognitive overload.
3. **Missing Conversion Path**:
   - The absence of a prominent quote or inquiry button means prospects who wade through the text wall still have no simple, frictionless way to initiate a conversation.
