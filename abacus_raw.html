Services We Offer

Tradesman Accounts - Your Partner in the Trades

Whether you're a one-man band or a large construction firm, our tradesman accounting services cover all your needs. From setting up your company to managing payroll, VAT, and tax planning, our tradesman accounts team has got you covered.

Business Accounts - Tailored for Your Industry

Our business accounting services are adaptable to any industry, with a special focus on sectors like healthcare, IT, and e-commerce. Our business accounts team can help you with company setup, payroll, VAT returns, tax planning, and more.

HMRC Investigations - Protecting Your Interests

Facing an HMRC investigation? Our HMRC investigations specialists can challenge HMRC figures, deal with complaints, and even represent you at tribunal if necessary. We provide the background work needed to ensure a fair outcome.

VAT Investigations - Navigating Complexities

VAT investigations require precision. Our VAT investigations team can help you challenge HMRC's figures, handle enquiries, and prepare the necessary background work, all backed by our CTA qualified specialist.

How to Set Up a Company - Your Business Blueprint

Confused about setting up a limited company? Our team can advise on all aspects of how to set up a company, calculate the best structure for you, assist with agreements, and handle everything from start to finish, including capital gains tax calculations and incorporation relief.

Overdue Tax Returns - Correcting Mistakes

Don't let overdue tax returns stress you out. We can update previous returns, appeal fines or penalties, and ensure HMRC assessments are accurate, even if it means redrafting them.

S162 Incorporation Relief - Tax Efficiency

Explore the benefits of S162 incorporation relief with us. Our S162 incorporation relief experts will guide you through this tax-saving strategy, helping you make the most of your business structure.

Xero Accountant - Streamline Your Finances

If you use Xero for your business, we can help you set it up efficiently and make the most of its features. As your Xero accountant, we can manage or advise on all aspects of set up, management and integrations.
