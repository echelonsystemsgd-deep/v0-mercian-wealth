Most businesses come to us when they no longer feel they’re getting the advice and support they need from their current accountant. Their queries aren’t answered quickly, they no longer feel like a priority and there’s a lack of proactive advice on how to legitimately save on their tax bill. Heck, some businesses have even lost trust in their existing accountant as they don’t have the information they need to make management decisions.

We can help you:

Pay Less Tax

Most owner-managed businesses aren’t getting the proactive tax planning advice they need. Meaning they’re losing out and paying the tax man more than they should! Through proactive advice throughout the year, we help business owners take advantage of tax efficiencies, like tax breaks and deductions, so they keep more of their hard-earned money. Whether it’s VAT returns, corporation tax, or personal tax returns, our tax advisers can help to find you savings, and make sure you never pay more tax than necessary (or less than you should!).

Get Back Time

As a small business owner, you know that time is money. So, it makes sense that you need to divert your time and attention away from complicated financial matters and into growing your business. At Raw, we understand how valuable time is to any busy business owner, which is why we have a range of accountant services designed to give you back your time through optimising and streamlining your accounting, bookkeeping and tax calculation processes.

Deliver Peace of Mind

For limited company owners, managing finances can be a daunting task. There are payroll taxes to account for, invoices to keep track of, and a myriad of other financial responsibilities. However, our accountancy services can take some of the burden off of your shoulders. We can handle all of the corporate accounting responsibilities of your business and provide you with peace of mind by ensuring that all your financial affairs are cost-effective, compliant, and in-order.

Who we help

We work with businesses in a range of industries and sectors, but have particular specialisms supporting the following types of businesses. If that’s what you do, then we’d love to hear from you and show you our specialisms supporting these sectors.  

Construction, trades and property developers

For construction and trades there are a number of unique challenges and requirements. As specialist construction accountants, our qualified accountants help construction businesses, contractors/subcontractors and property developers to keep on top of their finances and tax affairs.

Owner managed businesses looking to save tax

There are few certainties in life – but tax is one! As a business owner, you have flexibility on how to arrange your tax affairs. Through their timely advice, our experienced accountants can help identify tax opportunities to reduce your tax bill and keep you on the right side of the tax man!

Consultants, agencies and professional services

Running an agency or consultancy is great – but dealing with the accounting and tax can be a pain. We work with agencies and consultants delivering tailored business advice that gets their accounting set up for success, takes the pain away from time-consuming data entry and makes sure their tax affairs are structured right.
