FAQ

Why do I need to choose a qualified accountant?

Qualified accountants are:

  • Regulated by their regulatory bodies
  • have insurances in place
  • required to update themselves in a regular basis by the regulatory body through continuing professionals development activities

You can contact their regulatory body in case if anything goes wrong and couldn’t be sorted out between yourselves. So, you will have big peace of mind working with them in terms of quality of work and risks cover.

How do I know if an accountant is qualified?

Not easy for all but you can ask their regulatory body over the phone or go to their regulatory bodies’ website, you should be able to find out.

For us, being ACCA qualified member, you can find out using following link:

Here, you can find out whether an individual is qualified or not as well as whether the firm is licenced or not.
