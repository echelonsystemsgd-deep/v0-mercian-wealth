A Personal Letter From Neelam

"From My Kitchen To Yours — Why We Created Uttam Rasoi"

Namaste, I am Neelam. For years on my YouTube channel, hundreds of you commented asking: "Neelam ji, what garam masala do you use in your paneer?" or "How does your yellow dal get that rich dhaba fragrance without artificial essences?"

The honest truth is, I never used commercial supermarket spices. When we looked into factory-packaged spices, we were shocked to discover spent spice residue, artificial red dyes (Sudan dyes), anti-caking silica powders, and starch fillers used to cut costs.

In Indian culture, food isn't just fuel — it is Prasad. It is health, love, and our family’s immunity. So my husband Saurabh and I decided to bring the exact stone-ground spices we make for our own children directly to your doorstep.

Zero Chemical Milling

Cold stone grinding at slow RPMs leaves essential oils intact.

Direct From Farms

Nagaur methi, Meghalaya turmeric & Kashmiri chillies.

Daily Tested In Home

Every single batch is cooked in Neelam’s kitchen before packing.
