FAQs

What makes you different to other accountancy firms?

We deliver more than your standard accounting services. Sure, we’ll help manage your finances, but we’ll also help you understand them properly – so you can make better decisions with confidence.

Part of that comes from being a Xero Platinum Partner. You’ll benefit from market-leading cloud accounting expertise and efficient financial management. We’re also accredited by the Good Business Charter and committed to the SME Climate Hub, as part of our social commitment to ethical, responsible business practices.

How do you determine your fees?

We offer fixed fees, which means you know exactly what you’ll be paying for our services from the outset. This ensures that you receive consistent, high-quality support without unexpected charges. 

How secure is my financial information?

We take data security very seriously. Your financial information is stored securely, and we comply with all relevant data protection regulations. To ensure the highest level of security, we partner with a reputable security firm that continuously monitors and protects our systems against potential threats.

How do I switch my accounts over to you?

Switching is straightforward. We’ll handle the transition process and liaise with your previous accountant to ensure a smooth handover.

How often will you communicate with me?

We maintain regular communication with our clients, providing updates and advice as needed. At A&C Chartered Accountants, we believe that trust and communication are crucial to a successful partnership. You can reach out to us anytime for support, and we are always here to ensure you have the information and guidance you need to make informed decisions.

Where to find us?

We have two locations in Greater Manchester. If you would like to meet in-person, we’re happy to welcome you at our office in Manchester city centre. It’s on King Street, just 5 minutes walk from St. Peters’ Square. Or, for those based in Altrincham, Chorlton, Didsbury, Stretford, or Urmston, our accountants in Sale are easy to reach, with on-street car parking next to the office.
