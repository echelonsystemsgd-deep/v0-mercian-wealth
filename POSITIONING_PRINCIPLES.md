# Standing Rule: Outcome Over Tool, Always
**Mercian Wealth · Core Positioning, Messaging & Communication Protocol**

---

## 1. The Core Rule
- **Never lead with what the system is** (`AI`, `automation`, `website`, `workflow`, `agent`, `telephony`).
- **Always lead with what changes for the business**:
  1. **Time saved** (less admin drag, zero manual chasing)
  2. **Cost reduced** (fewer wasted ad clicks, no unneeded front-desk overhead)
  3. **Revenue increased** (more bookings closed, lost customers recaptured, upfront deposits secured)
  4. **Risk removed** (zero downtime, zero unvetted replies, keeping existing domains/calendars)

---

## 2. Sequencing, Not Secrecy
- **Outreach Openers**: Lead 100% with the outcome, never the mechanism.
  - *Winning Opener*: **"We help local businesses stop losing jobs to whoever replies first."**
  - *Follow-up Sequence*: Outcome opener → Customer reacts/asks ("How does that work?") → Explain briefly → Working prototype link does the heavy lifting.
- **Site Hero**: Lead with the commercial outcome first; the mechanism (the high-converting website/storefront) can follow a beat later in the subhead, because the visitor already knows they arrived on a website.
- **Naming the Tool**: The tool is named only after the prospect is already leaning in or has asked. Hiding it forever looks evasive once interest is established; leading with it invites instant resistance (*"I already have a website"*).

---

## 3. Voice & Tone Discipline
- **Universal Register**: The company voice is **"we"** across the site, documentation, pricing, and system operations.
- **Founder Spotlight**: First-person singular (**"I"**) is strictly confined to the founder commitment card:
  > *"I don't talk about agents, models, or workflows — that's our job, not yours. I talk about what changes for you: faster response times, fewer missed leads, more bookings closed, less time spent chasing admin. If a system doesn't save time, cut cost, grow revenue, or remove a risk, it doesn't go on your site."*

---

## 4. CTA Architecture
- **Single Universal Primary CTA**: **`"Book a call"`**
  - Used consistently across Navbar, Hero, System Breakdowns, Pricing, and Closing CTA.
  - Eliminates conflicting labels like *"Book 15-Min Audit"*, *"Book Consultation"*, or *"Get Started"*.
- **Secondary Actions (Visually Demoted)**:
  - Prototype Request Modal (`"See a free prototype of your site"`) — outline styling.
  - Direct WhatsApp priority link — subtle text/badge styling.

---

## 5. Absolute Proof Integrity (Zero Fabricated Metrics)
- **Verified Client Data**: If real numbers exist (e.g. The Caker Street Bakery: *499 hours saved, +£32,400/yr*), state them as verified facts.
- **Unproven / Prospective Work**: Never fabricate metrics or invent test percentages. Label them with complete honesty:
  - Badge: `OUTREACH PROTOTYPE · ZERO FABRICATED METRICS` (e.g. Aura Private Aesthetics clinic prototype).
- **Physical Grounding**: Real regional roots (*"Based in the West Midlands, serving businesses across the UK"*). Never invent fake company registration numbers or artificial office addresses.

---

## 6. Pre-Flight Checklist
Before sending any message, publishing any copy, or deploying any section, ask:
1. *Does this headline or opening sentence name a tool/mechanism before establishing the outcome?* If yes, invert it.
2. *Does this invite an instant "I already have one" objection?* If yes, refocus on the cost of slow response or leaked leads.
3. *Is every primary button labeled exactly "Book a call"?*
4. *Are all numbers either verifiably real or explicitly labeled as prototypes?*
